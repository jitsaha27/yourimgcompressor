Image Resizer & PDF Converter FAQs

How do I resize a photo for a passport or ID application?

Choose Photo & File Resizer, upload an image, enter the required width and height in centimeters or millimeters, then preview and download the resized file. Check your application’s exact size and format rules before submitting.

Can I combine multiple photos into one PDF?

Yes. Choose Image to PDF and select one or more JPG, PNG, or WEBP images. The tool places each image on its own A4 page while preserving its proportions.

How do I convert a PDF to JPG images?

Choose PDF to JPG, select a PDF, and convert its pages. The resulting JPG files are packaged together in a ZIP download.

Are my photos or documents uploaded to a server?

No. Image and PDF processing happens locally in your browser; your selected files are not sent to an application server.

Does the target file size setting guarantee an exact KB size?

No. It selects an estimated JPEG quality level, so the final file size varies with the image. Review the downloaded file against your form’s file-size limit.
